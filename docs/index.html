Questions

Before you try it

What do I need to run it?

The Claude app with a paid plan and skills turned on. You upload the skill once, then attach quotes in any chat.

Does it pick the winning bid?

No. It reads the quotes and flags gaps. You add your own costs for the gaps and make the call.

How accurate is it?

It only marks something Included when the quote says so, and shows you the line it relied on. When it isn't sure, it marks Not mentioned so you check.

Where do my quotes go?

Nowhere new. Everything happens in your own Claude account, and the sheet is saved to your computer.
