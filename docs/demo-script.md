# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record your screen in the Claude app. No face needed. Speak slowly.

**0:00 – The problem (show three quote PDFs side by side)**
"Three electrical quotes for the same job. Three different formats. One leaves out permits, one quietly limits temp power to ten weeks, one doesn't mention fire alarm monitoring. Lining these up by hand takes an hour per trade."

**0:20 – Drop them in**
Attach the three sample PDFs. Type: "Level these electrical bids for Riverside Clinic. It's an interior remodel, no site work."

**0:35 – Claude's answer**
Scroll the short summary: base prices, gap counts, the three gaps that matter most.

**0:55 – Open the sheet**
Summary tab first. Then the Leveling tab: point at the colors. Hover the Keystone permit cell to show the quote line ("Permit fees (will be billed at cost)").

**1:20 – Make it fair**
Type an adjustment for Keystone's permit fees and Brightline's temp power. Switch back to Summary and show the adjusted totals change.

**1:40 – Questions tab**
"And here are the questions to send each sub before bid day."

**1:50 – Close**
"It's free for electrical. Link's below. The full kit covers 20+ trades."
